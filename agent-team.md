# Agentic organization

To build the an agentic organization we need agents that learn while they work so they can adapt to circustances, remember what they have done and what they have learned, and take decision based on long term goals and not just short term rewards.

Those agents need to have a specific domain and especialize in it, so they can be more efficient and effective. Tasks need to be decomposed in a way that they can be assigned to the right agents, and those agents need to be able to communicate and collaborate with each other to achieve the overall goals of the organization.

## Agents

### Organization

These are top level agents that work across teams and projects. They are responsible for the overall strategy, vision, and direction of the organization. They are the ones who set the goals and objectives for the organization, and they are the ones who make sure that the organization is aligned with its mission and values.

#### Product Owner

It's the person responsible for the product, its vision, and its success. They are the ones who define the product roadmap, prioritize features, and make sure that the product meets the needs of the customers. It's the agent that talks to the human stakeholders and translates their needs into actionable tasks for the rest of the organization.

#### Project Manager

The project manager is responsible for planning, executing, and closing projects. They are the ones who define the project scope, create the project plan, and manage the project resources. They are the ones who make sure that the project is completed on time, within budget, and with the desired quality.

### Project team

These are the agents that work on specific projects and tasks. They are mini organizations in themselves that work together to achieve the goals of the project. They are responsible for the execution of the project and the delivery of the results.

### Architect

The architect is responsible for the overall design and structure of the project. They receive the requirements from the product owner and create specifications and guidelines for the rest of the team on how to implement them. They know the goals and the contsraints of the project, and they make sure that the implementation is aligned with them. It creates the blueprint and specifications on how things need to work. It doesn't know about implementation details but have a deep understanding on how things work at a high level.

### Developer

The developer is responsible for the implementation of the project. They receive the specifications from the architect and create the code that implements them. They are responsible of the implementations and own the changes to the codebase.

### Reviewer

The reviewer is responsible for reviewing the code and make sure that it follows the specifications and the best practices, ensuring that the code is consistent, maintainable, and of high quality. They need to maintain documentation on what are the patterns, conventions and best practices that the team follows and raise issues when they are not followed.

### Validator

The validator is responsible for testing the code and make sure that it works as expected. They create test cases and run them to ensure that the code is correct and that it meets the requirements. They are responsible that the project is working as expected and that it is of high quality without errors.
